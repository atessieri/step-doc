CPM Message
===========

Cooperative Perception Messages (CPMs) are messages exchanged in the ITS network between ITS-Ss
to extend each station's awareness of its surroundings beyond what it can directly perceive itself.
A CPM contains the reference time and position of the originating ITS-S, together with a list of
objects detected by its perception sensors (e.g. other vehicles, pedestrians, cyclists or obstacles).
For each perceived object the message includes its measurement time, position, speed, heading,
dimensions, an indication of the perception quality and, where available, a predicted future path.
On reception of a CPM the receiving ITS-S becomes aware of objects in the vicinity of the originating
ITS-S, including objects outside its own sensor range or line of sight. The received information can
be used by the receiving ITS-S to support several ITS applications. For example, by comparing the
perceived objects with its own knowledge of the environment, a receiving ITS-S is able to estimate
the collision risk with objects it may not otherwise detect and, if necessary, inform the driver of
the vehicle via HMI or in-vehicle system.
