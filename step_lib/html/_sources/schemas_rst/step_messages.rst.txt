STEP Messages
=============

The STEP messages are the messages defined by the STEP platform itself, as opposed to
the ETSI ITS messages (CAM, CPM, DENM). They are not wrapped by the ITS message
envelope and have their own JSON schemas.
The messages supported are as follows:

    * **Position**: Defines the structure for a single GNSS position fix reported
      by a station
