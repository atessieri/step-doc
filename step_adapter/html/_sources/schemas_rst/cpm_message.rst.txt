CPM Message
===========

Collective Perception Messages (CPMs) are messages exchanged in the ITS network between ITS-Ss to
share the objects perceived by the local sensors of the originating ITS-S. A CPM contains the
reference time, station id and reference position of the originating ITS-S and the list of its
perceived objects. The STEP Adapter converts every perceived object into one position array, from
its measured position and its predicted path.
